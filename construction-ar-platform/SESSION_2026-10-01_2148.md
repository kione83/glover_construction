## Limitations and incomplete requirements

- Surface recognition requires ARKit to classify an observed floor, wall or
  ceiling. Unclassified/weakly tracked targets are rejected with guidance.
  Stability and recognition usability require supported-iPhone acceptance.
- Manual two-reference alignment is still required in a fresh scanned-room AR
  session. This work does not implement automatic world-map relocalization.
- Fit checks use approximate semantic envelopes. Walls, openings, meshes and
  uncaptured obstacles are not checked, and clearances are not code approval.
- Alternatives are independent snapshots, not version history or a comparison UI.
- Products remain generic procedural representations; FR-11/19A realism is partial.
- Rescan-in-place is missing. A replacement capture has a new coordinate frame;
  preserving placements/connections requires explicit registration and safe
  archive replacement, not simply overwriting the original scan.
- Failed note/photo/AR edits are retained in memory, not a durable force-quit-safe
  draft store. Portable project backup/restore is missing.
- The stream remains a local, single-viewer prototype without TURN/authentication;
  it is not a composited-AR broadcast or production collaboration system. The
  standalone publisher and embedded publisher remain separate implementations.
- Prior-session dependency advisories remain follow-up work; dependencies were
  unchanged and the audit was not rerun in this implementation session.
- No tests/build regressions remain from the selected waves. Physical-device and
  two-device network acceptance remain explicitly unverified.
